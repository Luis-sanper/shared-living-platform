# Shared Living Platform

Semester project for Software Development Methodologies.

## Product idea

A platform for people living in shared accommodation to organize household responsibilities and participate in social activities.

## Product goal

Help people living in shared accommodation manage household responsibilities and connect with others in one platform.

## Target users

- People living in shared flats.
- Students and international students.
- Landlords or property managers with limited access.

## Main features

- Manage flats and room assignments.
- Create, assign, and complete household tasks.
- Communicate between tenants and the landlord.
- Upload evidence of completed tasks.
- Manage shared and private household storage.- Report and track maintenance issues.
- Create and join social activities.

## Main user journeys

- Flatmate household journey: join a flat, access an assigned room, manage shared responsibilities, and view household information inside the private flat space.
- Landlord journey: create and manage a flat, assign tenants to rooms, create mandatory tasks, and follow maintenance issues.
- Social journey: discover, create and join activities.

## Initial risks

- The project scope may become too large for one semester.
- Image upload may add technical complexity.
- Different user roles may make permissions more complex.

## Out of scope

- Real-time chat.
- Payments.
- Real-time maps.
- AI recommendations.
- Advanced social-network features.
