# Individual Project Agreement

Since this project is being developed individually, this agreement defines the working rules I will follow throughout the development process.

## Work organization

I will prioritize tasks that allow me to maintain a mostly linear development workflow. My goal is to create a clear path toward the final product by completing small objectives one after another.

Whenever possible, I will work on only one task at a time. However, closely related tasks may be developed partially in parallel when this makes the workflow more efficient.

## Task completion

A task will be considered completed when it satisfies the relevant acceptance criteria and the conditions established in the Definition of Done.

## Handling blockers

If I become blocked on a task, I will stop and look for advice or assistance that can help me understand and solve the problem.

If the problem prevents further progress, I may temporarily continue with another independent part of the project. If a task proves excessively complex or beyond the realistic scope of the project, I will reconsider its design or reduce its scope.

## Review and integration

Changes will only be merged into the main branch after I have reviewed and tested them sufficiently to demonstrate that they work as expected.

## Recording changes and decisions

Major changes that significantly affect the project direction will be explicitly documented.

Smaller changes will be reflected through commits and updates to the relevant documentation, preserving enough history to understand why the project evolved in a particular direction.

## Managing complex tasks

Large or complex tasks will be divided into smaller and more manageable pieces whenever possible. These tasks must remain appropriate to the scope of the project and the capabilities and resources available for its development.

## Work in progress limit

Whenever possible, only one task will be kept in progress at a time. Closely related tasks may be worked on simultaneously when separating them would make the development process less efficient.
