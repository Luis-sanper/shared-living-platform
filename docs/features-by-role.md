## Features by role

# Landlord

### Flat management
- Create flats
- Create and manage rooms inside a flat
- Invite tenants to specific available rooms
- Remove tenants from the flat
- View the list of tenants in the flat and their rooms

### Household tasks
- Create mandatory household tasks
- Require photo evidence for specific tasks
- View household tasks when included in their visibility settings
- View the completion status of mandatory tasks

### Maintenance
- View maintenance issues
- Update maintenance issue status

### Communication
- Send messages to the whole flat or to specific tenants

---------------------------------------------------------------

# Tenant

### Flat access
- Accept flat invitations
- View the flat and room information before accepting and invitation
- Leave the flat
- View basic flat information
- View the assigned room

### Household tasks
- Create household tasks and define their visibility settings
- Assign household tasks to themselves or other tenants in the same flat
- View assigned household tasks
- Complete household tasks assigned to them
- Upload photo evidence when required
- Create or receive task reminders

### Household items
- Manage shared and private household storage
- View shared household items
- View personal private items
- Add, edit, or remove shared household items
- Add, edit, or remove personal private items

### Maintenance
- Report maintenance issues
- View maintenance issue status

### Social activities
- Create public social activities
- Edit or cancel own social activities
- Join public social activities
- Leave an activity after joining
- View participants in an activity

### Communication
- Send messages in the shared flat-landlord conversation
- Send messages to the landlord
